Acknowledgements & Data Sources

HanziDrip is built on open data. This page lists what we use, the license each piece comes under, and what we changed.

HSK word lists

The HSK levels and word lists in HanziDrip follow the HSK vocabulary syllabus published by the Center for Language Education and Cooperation (CLEC) / Chinese Testing International, which runs the HSK exam. We digitised those lists using these open-source projects:

  • complete-hsk-vocabulary by Yanis Zafirópulos — the base HSK 2.0 and 3.0 word lists, with parts of speech and word-frequency rankings. Used under the MIT License.
  • hsk30 by Pleco Inc. — the HSK 3.0 lists that project builds on. MIT License.
  • hsk-vocabulary by Clement Venard — the HSK 2.0 lists that project builds on. MIT License.
  • HSK-3.0 by Mani (krmanik) — the word-frequency rankings that set the order of new words within each level, drawing on the SUBTLEX-CH film-subtitle corpus (Cai & Brysbaert, 2010). Used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License; we use the rankings only to order words within a level.

Dictionary definitions

English definitions are adapted from CC-CEDICT, the community-maintained Chinese–English dictionary (continuing Paul Denisowski's CEDICT project; hosted by MDBG). CC-CEDICT is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License.

Changes we made: we selected the senses shown for each HSK word, corrected readings and meanings, reformatted entries, and translated the definitions into Thai. As the license requires, our adapted definitions (English and Thai) are made available under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

HanziDrip's own content

The grammar notes, example sentences, learning-path lessons and Thai-language explanations were written for HanziDrip and are not part of the datasets above.
