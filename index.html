Common questions

How does the daily word feature work?
HanziDrip surfaces a rotating set of vocabulary words based on your HSK level and progress, both in the app and via the home screen widget.

Does my progress sync across devices?
Yes — your learning progress and daily word history sync privately through your own iCloud account, so they carry over between your devices signed into the same Apple ID.

What does Premium unlock?
Premium features are purchased and managed entirely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Do I need an account?
No. HanziDrip doesn't require sign-up or a username/password — it works entirely with your device and your private iCloud account.
